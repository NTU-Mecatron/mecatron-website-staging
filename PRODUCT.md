# Mecatron

Mecatron is a Singapore-based student marine robotics team. This site is a static, GitHub Pages-ready home for communicating what the team builds and why it matters.

**Last updated:** 2026-09-12  
**Documentation review interval:** 30 days  
**Full maintenance guide:** [`docs/SITE_GUIDE.md`](docs/SITE_GUIDE.md)

## Audience and job

Curious students, potential collaborators, and technical visitors should understand the team in under a minute, see a concrete vehicle/project, and have an easy path into ongoing field notes.

## Constraints

The repository must deploy as a static site. Team members should be able to add content in Markdown or MDX without touching page layout code. The template must be easy to rebrand with a team name, links, and real project imagery.

## Content model

Field logs use frontmatter for title, description, date, category, reading time, and featured state. The collection supports `Field log`, `Build note`, and `Team note`.
