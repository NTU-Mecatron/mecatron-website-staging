## The problem

Visibility dropped below two metres on day three. Instead of asking the pilot to fly closer to the reef, we changed what the vehicle considered useful evidence.

## What changed

We paired forward-looking sonar with a lower-confidence visual pass. The result was a slower but more honest map: fewer beautiful frames, more continuous shape.

## The takeaway

Autonomy is not about removing the ocean from the equation. It is about giving the team better ways to work with what the ocean is telling us.
