## Curious enough to go deeper.

We are a cross-disciplinary group of students who believe the best way to understand a difficult environment is to build something that can spend time there.

Our team brings together executive leadership, business operations, mechanical design, electrical and embedded systems, robotics software, and digital software. We learn by sharing the work and testing our assumptions in the real world.

The current team structure and approximate team sizes are maintained in `data/teams.yaml`, while every member profile is maintained once in `data/members.csv`. Yearly division assignments are maintained in `data/members-by-year.yaml`.
