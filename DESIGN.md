## Direction

The site is a field log, not a generic team brochure. It uses the visual language of a survey console translated into a warm editorial layout: deep ocean ink, sea-foam type, survey-orange signals, grid lines, and compact instrument labels.

## Tokens

- Ink: `#07151b`; soft ink: `#10272e`
- Paper: `#e8eee8`; dim paper: `#aabbb4`
- Signal orange: `#ff6b3d`; signal soft: `#ff9671`
- Foam: `#c5efe5`
- Display/body: IBM Plex Sans
- Measurement metadata: DM Mono

## Composition

Large declarative headings lead each section. Content uses open grids and hairline rules rather than nested cards. The homepage proves the work with a schematic vehicle visual, then routes visitors to MDX-powered field logs and the team story.
